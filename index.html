Rally Clock

A free timing and log app for rally control workers and marshals. It keeps an accurate clock, records car numbers, times and comments, and hands the log to whoever is scoring — so nobody retypes a paper sheet at midnight. Out now for Android on Google Play and for Linux, and coming soon to iPhone, iPad and Mac.
