Privacy Policy

Applies to Rally Clock and other apps published by NitroCat Innovations.
Effective September 25, 2026.

The short version: our apps keep your data on your device. We don't have accounts, we don't run servers that receive your data, and we don't use analytics, advertising, or tracking of any kind. We never sell or share your data.

What the app stores

Rally Clock saves the information you enter — event setup, car numbers, times, comments, and your settings — in the app's private storage on your device. It stays there until you delete it or uninstall the app.

Location

Rally Clock uses your device's location only for features you turn on:

  • GPS position stamp — one reading, taken only when you tap for it.
  • Trip meters — while a trip meter is running, the app reads your location to measure distance, including with the screen locked. A notification shows whenever this is happening, and it stops when you stop the trip meter.

Location is processed on your device. It is not sent to us or to anyone else, except as part of a log you choose to send (see below).

When data leaves your device

Only when you act, and only to places you choose:

  • Sharing or handing over a log — to another device running Rally Clock on the same local network, or through your phone's share menu (email, messaging, files, and so on). Once shared, that copy is handled by the recipient and the app you shared it with.
  • Loading an event setup from another device on your local network.
  • Setting the clock — the app asks a public internet time server (by default pool.ntp.org, changeable in Settings) for the current time. Like any internet request, this reveals your device's IP address to that server. No other data is sent.

What we collect

Nothing. NitroCat Innovations does not receive, collect, or store any personal information from our apps. If you email us, we use your message and address only to reply.

Permissions

Location, local network access, and notifications are requested only when a feature needs them, and you can revoke them at any time in your device settings. The rest of the app keeps working without them.

Deleting your data

Because your data lives only on your device, you control it completely: delete logs within the app, clear the app's data in your device settings, or uninstall the app. There is no account to close.

Children

Our apps are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above. If a future version of an app collects data in a new way, this policy will say so before that version is released.
